1. Add `https://2026itstime.com/` as a property in Google Search Console.
2. Verify ownership using the verification method Google gives you.
3. Submit `https://2026itstime.com/sitemap.xml` in the Sitemaps report.
4. Inspect `https://2026itstime.com/` with URL Inspection and choose **Request indexing**.
5. After major content changes, request re-indexing of the homepage again.
